Neon Runner 3D Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what the Neon Runner 3D game collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers the Android app on Google Play and the web version of the game.

The game is made and run by Roye Grinfeld ("we", "us"). Contact: neonrunner3d@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • The game shows no ads and does not use your advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with other companies for their own use.
  • To save your progress and show the leaderboards, the game creates a guest account for you automatically. Signing in with email or Google is optional.
  • From version 3.3, a new install sends anonymous performance and progress information during its first session only, so we can tell whether new players leave because the game runs badly on their phone or because something in the game is unclear.

2. What we collect

Guest account (automatic)

When the game first starts, it creates a guest account with a random user ID and a guest name (for example RUNNER_1234). It is used to save your progress and to list your scores on the leaderboards.

Account you choose to create (optional)

If you sign up with email, we store your email address and the display name you choose. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email, your name and a link to your Google profile picture, and a Google account identifier. This lets you sign in and keep your progress on other devices.

Gameplay data

Scores, distances, coins earned and spent, levels and cities completed, runners you own and use, missions and achievements, and the dates of your runs. Leaderboards are public: they show your display name or guest name, your score, the city and your runner icon.

First-session information (new installs only, version 3.3 and later)

During the very first session after you install the game, it sends: your phone's manufacturer and model, Android version, memory size class (for example "4GB"), graphics chip name, Android WebView version and app version; how long the game took to start and to show the first picture; the frame rate while loading and during your first run; which first-session steps you reached (main menu, start of the run, first obstacle, first hit, end of the run, second run); and the screen that was showing when the game was closed or sent to the background.

This uses a random ID made for that one session. It is not linked to your account, your guest account or any device identifier, and we do not use it to identify you. Players who update from an earlier version are not included, and nothing is sent after the first session.

Billboard sponsor requests

If you ask to sponsor an in-game billboard, we store the brand name, contact email, link and image you submit, so we can review the request.

Server logs

Like any online service, our backend provider records standard server logs, such as IP address and time of each request, for security and reliability. We do not use them to track you.

3. What stays on your device

Settings, progress and best scores are also kept in the game's storage on your device so it works offline. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

4. How we use it

  • Run the game, save your progress and show the leaderboards
  • Sign you in, if you choose to create an account
  • Check submitted scores to prevent cheating
  • Find and fix performance problems and parts of the game that new players find confusing
  • Review billboard sponsor requests
  • Answer your messages

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data or share it with other companies for their own purposes. We use these service providers, which process data for us:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Google: Google Play (app distribution, in-app purchases if offered, and the Play rating dialog) and Google Sign-In if you choose it. If you buy something in the game, Google Play handles the payment and we never see your card details. A rating you leave in the Play dialog goes to Google Play, not to us.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Guest and account data: while your account exists. Deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.
  • First-session information: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Sponsor requests: while we review them and for as long as the billboard runs.
  • Server logs: for the provider's standard short period.

7. Delete your data and your other rights

To delete your account and game data, email neonrunner3d@gmail.com with your display name or guest name (and the email address of your account, if you have one). We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it.

First-session information is not linked to you, so we cannot find or delete it for a specific person; it is deleted automatically after 12 months. You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the game.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to stored data is restricted. First-session information can only be written by the game, never read back by it.

10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update this page and the date at the top. For important changes we also show a notice in the game.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: neonrunner3d@gmail.com.
